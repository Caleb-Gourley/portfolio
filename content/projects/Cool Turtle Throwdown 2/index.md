## Overview

An above-board fighting turtles league, where up to four turtles are thrown into an arena and need to take each other down to win.

The theme is "Random Title Generator", and we generated "Cool Turtle Throwdown 2", a top-down battler. Our core fantasy is turtles fighting other turtles in an arena, with egg-based shooters and an atmosphere like an MMA or Boxing arena, with crowds cheering.

## What I Built

- The local multiplayer, supporting up to four players.
- Customisation system allowing each player to choose their own turtle shell.
- Cycling through the different available maps.

## What I Learned

Cool Turtle Throwdown 2 was super exciting and a multidisciplinary team to work with. I really enjoyed the couch co-op and versus games as a medium, so it was fun to build support for multiple players, and I could see it expanding into a larger demo and being released on the university arcade machine.
